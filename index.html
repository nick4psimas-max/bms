What it is

A different kind of scholarship

The Balanced Man Scholarship is Sigma Phi Epsilon's flagship award, given each year to incoming men who show real strength across the board: in the classroom, in their character, and in how they carry themselves.

It's built on a simple idea SigEp has stood behind for over a century: Sound Mind and Sound Body. You don't have to join the fraternity to apply or to win. We're recognizing the kind of men who are already living this way, and we'd love to meet you.

What we look for

The Balanced Man

I
Sound Mind
Academic drive and intellectual curiosity. Men who take their growth seriously.
II
Sound Body
A commitment to health, discipline, and taking care of yourself and those around you.
III
Character & Leadership
Integrity, service, and the willingness to step up. Leading by example, not title.
